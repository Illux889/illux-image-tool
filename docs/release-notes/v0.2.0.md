## What's new in 0.2.0

- **Resampling up and down, with bicubic.** Images are now resampled to a target ppi both when the resolution is too low and when it is too high, using bicubic (Photoshop's plain Bicubic). The ppi comes from size-based **Resolution rules** in the settings. Defaults: images that fit within 40 × 30 cm go to 300 ppi (left alone at 250-350 ppi); all other images go to 200 ppi (left alone at 150-250 ppi). The print size is always kept.
- **macOS support.** Apple silicon, macOS 15 or newer. The package is not signed: the first time, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. Watched folders on a Mac must be on the local disk (for example `/Users/Shared/...`).
- **Windows tray.** A single click now opens the settings (it was a double-click), and tray messages show as small windows.

Known limit: on a Mac, **Update now** is first exercised end to end on the release after 0.2.0. CI simulates it, but 0.2.0 is the first version that can update itself on macOS.

Files: `IlluxImageTool-Setup-0.2.0.exe` (Windows) and `IlluxImageTool-0.2.0-macOS-arm64.pkg` (macOS), each with a `.sha256` file.
